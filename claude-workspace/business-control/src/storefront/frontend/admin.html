Heatmap — where people click

Click positions are recorded as a fraction of the page, so the overlay lines up whatever screen the visitor used. No visitor ids and no typed text are stored — password and email fields are skipped entirely.

Page funnel

Which pages people land on, where they go next, and where they leave. Reconstructed from the pageview log — the step funnel in the ops app answers "how many bought"; this answers "which page leaks".

Blog comments

Comments are off by default and can be switched on per post in the Blog tab. New comments are held until you approve them — auto-publishing means you hear about spam from a customer.

Campaigns

One brief, running everywhere. Each campaign holds the cuts you're running per platform, a tracked /c/ link, and the orders its discount code actually brought in — read from your own ledger, not a platform's dashboard.
