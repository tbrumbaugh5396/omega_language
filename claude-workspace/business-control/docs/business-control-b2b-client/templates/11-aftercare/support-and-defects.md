## Defect, or change?

**A defect** is the delivered site not doing what it was signed off doing.
Free to fix under the plan, and under the warranty below without one.

**A change** is the site doing what it was signed off doing, and you wanting
something else. Quoted, or drawn from your included hours.

| Reported | Which | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Contact form stopped sending | **Defect** | It worked at sign-off |
| Contact form should also copy the sales inbox | Change | New behaviour |
| Menu breaks on iPhone | **Defect** | In the tested browser matrix |
| Menu breaks in [OLD BROWSER] | Change | Outside the matrix — quoted |
| Checkout errors after a plugin update | **Defect** | The update is ours |
| Checkout errors after a Client edit | Change | Fixed, then billed hourly |
| Price shows the wrong number | **Defect** if we typed it, change if you did |
| "Can we add a blog?" | Change | New functionality |

**The tested matrix** is [BROWSERS AND DEVICES], the versions current at
launch and their two predecessors. It is written here so neither of us has to
remember what we said in a meeting.

**Where it is genuinely unclear**, the Studio calls it a defect. Once. Arguing
about a twenty-minute fix costs more than the fix.

## Severity, and what each one buys

| | **What it means** | **Example** | **First response** | **Target fix** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **P1 — down** | Site unreachable, or money can't be taken | 500 on every page; checkout dead | **[1] hour**, in plan hours | **[4] hours**, work continues until resolved |
| **P2 — broken** | A core journey fails, no workaround | Contact form silently discards; login loops | **[4] hours** | **[1] working day** |
| **P3 — degraded** | Something is wrong, there is a workaround | One page mis-renders; slow gallery | **[1] working day** | **[5] working days** |
| **P4 — cosmetic** | Nothing is blocked | Wrong spacing; a typo we introduced | **[2] working days** | Next scheduled maintenance |

**The Studio sets the severity**, using the table, and tells you when it
differs from what you reported. Everything is P1 to the person who found it,
which is exactly why it can't be the reporter's call.

**First response** means a human who has looked, saying what it is and what
happens next — not an auto-acknowledgement, and not a fix. Targets run in
**business hours ([HOURS], [DAYS], excluding [HOLIDAYS])**, except P1 on the
Priority plan, which runs [24/7 | extended hours].

**Targets, not guarantees.** Where a fix depends on a third party — host,
payment provider, plugin author — the Studio chases it, keeps you posted, and
tells you plainly when it is out of our hands.

## Raising something

**One route: [SUPPORT EMAIL / PORTAL].** Not a text to a mobile, not a
comment in a document, not a message to whoever you met last. A request sent
anywhere else is not tracked, and the clock has not started.

A useful report — and it does speed up the fix:

1. What you did, in order
2. What you expected, and what happened instead
3. The URL, and a screenshot or screen recording
4. Browser and device, if you know
5. When it started, and whether anything changed around then

**We confirm receipt with a reference.** Quote it and everyone stays on the
same thread.

**Out of hours**, P1 on Priority goes to [EMERGENCY ROUTE]. Using it for a P3
is how it stops being answered.

## Without a care plan

Defects reported within **[30] days** of launch are fixed free. That is the
warranty in clause 15 of the agreement, and it is the whole of it.

After that, defects and changes alike are billed at **$[X]/hour**, [MINIMUM]
minimum, scheduled against work already booked — which in a busy month means
days, not hours. No response targets apply. This is not a penalty; it is what
"no plan" means, written down so it isn't a surprise on the day something
breaks.

## What support does not cover

- Problems caused by Client edits, other contractors, or third-party plugins
  installed outside the plan — diagnosed and fixed, then billed hourly
- Outages at the host, registrar, payment or email provider — chased on your
  behalf, not fixable by us
- Content writing, photography, data entry
- Training beyond the handover session — quoted separately
- Recovery from credentials shared or leaked outside the Studio
