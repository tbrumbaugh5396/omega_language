# Stage 10 — Keeping it, and getting paid for it
| Document | Goes to | When |
|---|---|---|
| `care-plan-agreement.md` | **Client** | Offered at handover, while goodwill is highest |
| `testimonial-request.md` | **Client** | 3–4 weeks after launch, once results exist |

Sell the care plan at handover. Two months later they've forgotten how much
they needed you, and a cold pitch for a maintenance contract is a much harder
conversation than a warm one on the day you handed over something that works.

Ask for the testimonial once there are numbers to quote. "It looks great" is
worth less than "enquiries up 40% in two months".
