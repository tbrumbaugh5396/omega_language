# Sales call questionnaire

45 minutes. Two thirds listening. Don't quote on the call.

**Client:** ______________  **Date:** ______  **Attendees:** ______________

---

## Opening (2 min)

> "Thanks for making time. I'd like to spend most of this understanding the
> business rather than talking about websites — then I'll go away and put
> something together. Sound OK?"

---

## 1. The business (10 min)

- What does the business do, in the words you'd use to a stranger?
- How do you make money — what's the main thing, what's the profitable thing?
- Who's the customer? Walk me through a typical one.
- How do they find you today?
- Who else does what you do, and why do people pick you instead?
- What's changing in the next year? _(new product, new market, hiring, funding)_

## 2. The current site (8 min)

- Where is it now? _(pull it up together)_
- What works about it?
- What makes you wince?
- What do customers or staff complain about?
- Who built it, and can you edit it yourself today?
- Any analytics? What do the numbers say?

> **Listen for:** "I can't update it myself" and "it looks dated" are surface
> symptoms. The real driver is usually further down.

## 3. Why now (5 min) — the most important section

- **Why now rather than last year or next year?**
- What happens if you don't do it?
- Is there a date this has to be done by? What's driving that?
- **What has to be true in twelve months for this to have been worth it?**

> No urgency = no project. Politely park it and follow up in a quarter.

## 4. Success and measurement (5 min)

- How will you know it worked? Be specific.
- What's a lead or a sale worth to you, roughly?
- How many do you get a month now? How many would be good?

> This is where the budget conversation gets easy. If a lead is worth $2,000
> and the site brings four more a month, a $15k site is obviously cheap.

## 5. Scope (8 min)

- How many pages, roughly?
- Anything beyond pages — booking, shop, members, calculators, integrations?
- Who writes the copy? _(if "you?" — that's a line item)_
- Do you have photography? Recent? Rights cleared?
- Do you have a logo and brand guidelines?
- Anything it must connect to? _(CRM, email, booking, accounting, POS)_
- Anything you've seen that you like? Why that one?

## 6. Decision and money (5 min)

- **Who else has to say yes?**
- How do decisions like this normally get made here?
- **What budget range are you working with?**

> If they deflect: *"Most projects like this land between $[X] and $[Y]
> depending on how much is custom. Does that sound like the right
> neighbourhood, or should I be thinking differently?"*

- Is the budget approved, or does it need approving?
- When would you want to start?

## 7. Close (2 min)

> "That's everything I need. I'll put together a proposal with three options
> so you can see the trade-offs — with you by [DAY]. Anything else I should
> know before I do?"

decision-maker, realistic budget, they've hired for this kind of thing before.
