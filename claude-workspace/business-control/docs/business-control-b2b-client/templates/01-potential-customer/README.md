# Stage 01 — Someone got in touch
An enquiry is not a project. This stage exists to find out which it is, fast,
without spending an afternoon on someone who was price-checking.

| Document | Goes to | When |
|---|---|---|
| `email-scripts.md` | Client | Reply within one working day |

**Reply same day if you can.** Speed of first reply correlates with winning the
work more than anything else you control at this stage — most studios take
three days, and the client has usually decided by then.

**What must come back before the next stage:** a booked call with a named
person and a rough sense of whether there is a budget at all.

**Gate:** they book the call. If they won't, they aren't a client yet — park
them and follow up in a quarter.
