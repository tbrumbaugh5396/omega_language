# Rate card & add-ons

Internal. Never send this to a client — they get a proposal with three
options, not a menu. This is how you build those options consistently.

Replace every number with your own. The structure matters more than the
figures.

## Core tiers

| Tier | What it is | Price | Timeline | Revisions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Guided setup** | We stand your install up, import what you have, configure the capabilities you picked and walk your team through it | $500 | 1 week | — |
| **Week website** | Existing template, your content, up to 5 pages, no custom work | $1,500 – $4,000 | 5 working days | 1 round |
| **Custom build** | Template foundation, custom sections, a custom design pass, 5–15 pages | $5,000 – $15,000 | 4–6 weeks | 2 rounds |
| **Custom build + Branding & creative** | Designed from scratch with the identity made alongside it: bespoke functionality, unlimited pages, motion system | $18,000 – $60,000 | 8–16 weeks | 2 rounds per phase |
| **Branding & creative** | Identity, art direction, motion, campaign assets — alongside any rung, or on its own | $6,000 – $40,000 | 4–10 weeks | 2 rounds per deliverable |

**Pricing rule:** price the tier, not the hours. Hourly punishes you for
getting faster and invites arguments about how long something "should" take.

## How to build the three options

Always three. The middle one is the one you expect them to take.

**Option A — the floor.** Solves the stated problem, nothing more. Priced so
it's plainly viable. Some clients need permission to spend less.

**Option B — the recommended.** What you'd actually do. Add the one or two
add-ons that most affect the outcome (usually copy and photography). Say
plainly: *"this is what I'd choose in your position, and here's why."*

**Option C — the everything.** B plus the ambitious pieces — extra rounds
bundled, integrations, a care plan pre-paid for a year. Prices B by comparison
even when nobody takes it. Some people take it.

Rough spacing: if B is $12k, A is around $7k and C around $22k.

## Payment terms by size

| Project value | Schedule |
|---|---|
| Under $5k | 100% up front, or 50/50 |
| $5k – $15k | 50% deposit, 50% before launch |
| $15k – $40k | 40% deposit, 30% at design sign-off, 30% before launch |
| Over $40k | 30% deposit, then monthly against milestones |

**Final payment before launch, never after.** Leverage disappears the moment
the site is live.

## Discounts — the only two worth giving

1. **Pre-paid annual care plan:** two months free on twelve. Buys retention
   and cash up front.
2. **Case study rights:** 5–10% off in exchange for a named case study, real
   numbers and a testimonial. You're buying marketing you'd otherwise pay for.

Never discount for "exposure", for a promise of future work, or because
someone asks. A discount given because someone asked teaches them to ask
again, and teaches you your price was fiction.
