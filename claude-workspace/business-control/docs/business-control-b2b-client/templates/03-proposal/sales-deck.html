05 · The selling machinery

Every step from "curious" to "paid" — handled.

Checkout that converts

  • Guest checkout — no forced account, no abandoned carts over a password
  • Card payments + pay-on-delivery — with an email-confirmation gate, so fake orders never count as revenue
  • One code field — discounts and gift cards in the same box; the system works out which is which
  • Free-shipping meter & upsells — "you're $0.52 away" sells the next can
  • Subscriptions — skip, pause or cancel — kept, not trapped

Growth built in

  • Affiliate links — every influencer gets a tracked link and a ledger
  • A/B testing — headlines and offers tested on real visitors, winners kept
  • Reviews with verified buyers — social proof you don't have to beg for
  • Promotions with countdowns — drops with real urgency
  • Analytics + click heatmap — see where attention actually goes — privacy-first, consent built in

06 · The app question

It's an app too — without the app store.

Zenjoy installs straight from the browser: an icon on the home screen, full-screen, fast. That's a progressive web app — one build that is both your website and your app.

What customers get

  • Install in two taps — from the shop itself; no store listing, no review queue
  • Works on bad connections — the shop shell keeps working offline and mid-deploy
  • Push notifications — drops and restocks land on the lock screen
  • Always current — no "please update the app"; everyone is on the latest version

What the business gets

  • No 30% platform cut — every sale is yours; there is no store between you and the customer
  • One codebase — a fraction of the cost of separate iOS and Android builds, forever
  • Staff devices too — the time clock and back office install on any phone or tablet — QR badge clock-in included
  • Ship daily — a fix or a promo goes live the moment it's ready, not after review

The quiet part: the offline shell is tested — including the case where a deploy lands mid-visit. Most PWAs break exactly there.

07 · The back office · ERP / CRM

Run the day from one screen.

Orders & stock

  • Order management — statuses, customer history, distributor case pricing
  • Inventory with low-stock alerts — know before you sell out, not after
  • Delivery routes — plan the truck, not just the order
  • Support hub — chat, tickets and call-backs land in the same queue

People & paper

  • Time clock — PIN or QR badge; PINs stored hashed, admin resets
  • Document vault + e-signature — contracts signed and archived where the work is
  • Audit log of every edit — who changed what, when — with retention rules
  • QR sign-in — staff on shared devices without shared passwords

08 · The back office · finance & supply

Numbers you can steer by.

Supply chain

  • Sourcing & suppliers — purchase orders, confirmations feeding receiving
  • Supplier portal — vendors confirm orders on a revocable link — no login to manage
  • Demand forecasting — what to order next, counting stock already on the way

Real margins

  • Bill of materials → true COGS — what a can of Mango Black Tea actually costs, not a guess
  • P&L fed by supply costs — margin per flavour, live
  • Your data, exportable — database viewer, one-click export and backups — never held hostage

The quiet rule behind all of it: revenue, costs and history are derived from one set of records — so the shop, the shelf and the books can't disagree.
