# Stage 05 — Gathering what you need to build
Four documents out at once, on day one. They take time to come back and they
are all on the critical path.

| Document | Goes to | When |
|---|---|---|
| `welcome-guide.md` | **Client** | Immediately after the deposit clears |
| `branding-questionnaire.md` | **Client** | With the welcome guide |
| `technical-questionnaire.md` | **Client** | With the welcome guide |
| `asset-checklist.md` | **Client** | With the welcome guide |
| `content-planner.md` | **Client** | With the welcome guide |
| `project-roadmap.md` | **Client** | Filled in with their real dates, then re-sent weekly |

**Content is the critical path, always.** Every late project was late because
copy and photographs didn't arrive. Agree a content deadline in writing on day
one and chase weekly from then on.

**Access is the other one.** DNS is the single thing that cannot be worked
around on launch day. If who-controls-the-domain is uncertain, resolve it this
week, not in week eight.

**The project roadmap is the one you keep sending.** Fill it with their real
dates at kickoff and re-send it every week for the rest of the project. It
costs ten minutes and it answers "how's it going?" before they have to ask —
which is the difference between a client who trusts the schedule and one who
starts checking up on you.

> Not to be confused with the studio's own [delivery
> roadmap](../../roadmap.md), which is internal. This one is theirs.

**Gate:** questionnaires returned, shared folder live, content deadline agreed
in writing, approver named, roadmap sent with real dates.
