## What we need from you

Everything here is on the critical path. A date that slips here moves launch by
the same amount — not because we're strict, but because the work genuinely
cannot proceed without it.

| What | Why it matters | Due | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Logo as a vector file (.ai / .svg) | The single most common cause of delay | [DATE] | ☐ |
| Photography — largest originals | We handle cutouts and sizing | [DATE] | ☐ |
| **The words** | Or add copywriting and we'll write them | [DATE] | ☐ |
| Domain and DNS access | The one thing that can't be worked around on launch day | [DATE] | ☐ |
| A payment account in your name | Money goes straight to you; we never touch it | [DATE] | ☐ |
| Exports from systems we're replacing | Some platforms make this slow — start early | [DATE] | ☐ |
| Feedback, consolidated, within 5 working days | Piecemeal feedback is how timelines die | Each round | ☐ |
| One decision-maker, ~2 hrs a week | [NAME] | Ongoing | ☐ |

> **Content is the critical path, always.** Every project that has ever run
> late ran late because copy and photographs arrived late. If writing isn't
> going to happen in-house, say so now — adding copywriting costs less than the
> delay it prevents.
