# Stage 04 — Signature and deposit
| Document | Goes to | When |
|---|---|---|
| `contracts/` — pick the tier | **Client** | Same day they choose an option |
| `contracts/common-clauses.md` | Internal reference | The spine all four share |

**Nothing starts without both a signature and a cleared deposit.** Not "we'll
sort the paperwork as we go". The deposit is the client deciding they're
serious, and every project that skipped it went badly in a way that was
predictable from this moment.

**Gate:** signed contract on file, deposit cleared in the bank, dates in the
schedule. Then, and only then, open stage 05.
