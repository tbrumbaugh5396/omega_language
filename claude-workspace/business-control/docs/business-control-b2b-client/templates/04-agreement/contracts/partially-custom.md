## 1. What this is

A website built on a **proven foundation** — an existing framework or theme —
with **custom design applied throughout** and **custom sections built** where
the standard components don't fit.

The distinction from Fully Custom: the underlying structure and patterns are
known-good and reused. The design, sections and content are yours.

not the proposal. Anything absent from it is a change order.

## 3. Revisions

**Two rounds of feedback are included**, deliberately different in purpose.

### Round 1 — homepage, direction

Presented when the homepage design is complete. Assesses **direction**: does
the design match the brand, is the structure right, does the message land.

Not the round for typos or final images — saying so up front prevents a
wasted pass on details that are about to change anyway.

### Round 2 — full site, polish

Presented when all pages are built. Copy corrections, image swaps, spacing,
links, mobile behaviour, browser issues.

### Rules for both

- **One consolidated response per round**, on the supplied form, within
  **[5] working days**.
- Feedback arriving piecemeal is collected and held until the round closes.
- Only **[NAMED APPROVER]** gives feedback (Common Clause 4).
- Reopening something approved in round 1 during round 2 is a change order.
- No response within **[10] working days** = approval; the project advances.
- **Additional rounds: $[X] each**, and may move the launch date.

> Two rounds is what almost every project of this size needs. The limit exists
> so a fixed price can exist — not to be stingy, and a third round is always
> available at the price above.
