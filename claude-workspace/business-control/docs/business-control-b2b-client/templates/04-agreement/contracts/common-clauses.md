# Common clauses

> **Not legal advice.** These are drafted from ordinary commercial practice
> and are a sound starting point. Have a lawyer in your jurisdiction read them
> once before you use them on real clients — particularly the payment, IP,
> revision-limit and liability sections. It's a one-off cost that protects
> every project afterwards.

The four contracts share this spine. Each tier document states its own scope,
price, timeline and revision count, then incorporates these by reference — or
paste them in, if you'd rather send one file.

---

## 1. Parties and agreement

This Agreement is between **[YOUR LEGAL NAME / COMPANY]** ("the Studio") and
**[CLIENT LEGAL NAME]** ("the Client"), effective **[DATE]**.

It covers the work described in the attached proposal dated [DATE], which
forms part of this Agreement. Where the proposal and this Agreement conflict,
this Agreement governs.

## 2. Payment

- Fees are as stated in the tier document. All amounts exclude [TAX].
- **Work begins when the deposit clears**, not when the Agreement is signed.
- Invoices are due **[14] days** from issue.
- Late payments accrue **[1.5]% per month** after [14] days.
- The Studio may pause work on any account more than **[21] days** overdue,
  and is not liable for delays caused by that pause.
- **Final payment is due before the site is made live.**
- Third-party costs (hosting, domains, licences, stock, fonts, plugins) are
  the Client's, billed at cost or paid directly by the Client.

## 3. Revisions and feedback

This clause is the one that keeps projects finite. It is not a penalty; it is
what allows a fixed price to exist.

- The tier document states the number of **feedback rounds included**.
- A round is **one consolidated set of feedback**, delivered on the form
  provided, within **[5] working days** of the work being presented.
- Feedback arriving piecemeal, or from parties not named in clause 4, will be
  collected and treated as part of the current round.
- Failure to respond within [10] working days constitutes approval, and the
  project moves to the next stage.
- Additional rounds are available at **$[X] per round** and may extend the
  timeline.
- Changes to work already approved in a previous round are a **change
  request** under clause 5, not feedback.

## 4. Approvals and authority

The Client names **[NAME, ROLE]** as the sole person authorised to give
feedback and approvals. The Studio will take direction from this person only.
The Client may change this by written notice.

## 5. Changes to scope

- Anything not described in the agreed requirements document is out of scope.
- Out-of-scope requests are quoted in a **written change order** stating cost
  and schedule effect, and require the Client's written approval before work
  starts.
- The Studio will not refuse reasonable changes; it will price them.

## 6. Client responsibilities

The Client will provide, by the dates in the project plan:
- All text content, or commission the Studio to write it
- Images and media, with rights cleared for the intended use
- Logo files, brand guidelines, and any existing assets
- Timely access to hosting, domain and third-party accounts
- Timely feedback and approvals per clause 3

**Delays and stalls.** Where materials are outstanding for more than **[14]
days**, the Studio may pause the project and reallocate the time. Where a
project is stalled more than **[30] days**, restarting is subject to
availability and a rescheduling fee of **$[X]**. Fees already paid are not
refunded on account of Client delay.

## 7. Intellectual property

- The Client warrants it owns or has licence to all materials it supplies, and
  indemnifies the Studio against claims arising from them.
- **On final payment**, the Client owns the delivered final designs and custom
  code produced for this project.
- The Studio retains ownership of its pre-existing tools, frameworks,
  libraries and templates, and grants the Client a perpetual, non-exclusive
  licence to use them as part of the delivered work.
- Working files, source design files and unused concepts remain the Studio's
  unless purchased separately at **$[X]**.
- Third-party assets remain governed by their own licences.
- Until final payment, all deliverables remain the Studio's property.

## 8. Portfolio rights

The Studio may display the work in its portfolio, case studies and marketing,
and may describe its role. The Client may request a **[6]-month** embargo in
writing. Confidential business information is never disclosed.

## 9. Warranties and defects

- The Studio will correct defects reported within **[30] days** of launch at
  no charge, where the defect is in work it produced.
- A defect is the work not functioning as specified. It is not a change of
  mind, a new requirement, or a third-party service changing its behaviour.
- The Studio does not warrant uninterrupted or error-free operation, nor any
  specific business outcome such as ranking, traffic or revenue.
- Browser support: current and previous major versions of Chrome, Safari,
  Firefox and Edge.

## 10. Liability

The Studio's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the fees paid
by the Client. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss,
including lost profit or data. Nothing limits liability for death, personal
injury, or fraud.

## 11. Confidentiality

Each party will keep the other's non-public information confidential and use
it only for this project. Survives termination by [2] years.

## 12. Termination

- Either party may terminate on **[14] days'** written notice.
- The Client pays for all work completed to the termination date, plus any
  committed third-party costs.
- Deposits are non-refundable — they reserve time that cannot be resold.
- On termination for cause by the Client, the Studio delivers work completed
  to date on payment of outstanding fees.

## 13. Independent contractor

The Studio is an independent contractor, not an employee, partner or agent.
The Studio controls its own methods, tools and hours, and may subcontract
while remaining responsible for the work.

## 14. General

- **Force majeure:** neither party is liable for delay caused by events beyond
  reasonable control.
- **Governing law:** [JURISDICTION].
- **Disputes:** the parties will attempt good-faith resolution, then mediation
  in [LOCATION], before proceedings.
- **Entire agreement:** this Agreement and the attached proposal are the whole
  agreement and supersede prior discussions.
- **Amendments** must be in writing and signed by both parties.
- **Severability:** if a provision is unenforceable, the rest stands.

## 15. Ongoing support — security, compliance, monitoring, updates

Software is not finished at launch; it is maintained or it decays. The
parties agree up front how the continuous work is carried:

**Covered under a Care Plan** (per the [care plan
agreement](../../11-aftercare/care-plan-agreement.md), when one is signed):
security patches and dependency updates, uptime and error monitoring,
backups and restore tests, bug and error support at the plan's response
times, and compliance upkeep the plan names ([WCAG / PCI scope / privacy
obligations — LIST]).

**Without a Care Plan:** the Studio warrants fixes for defects reported
within **[30] days** of launch at no charge. After that, security updates,
monitoring, bug fixes and compliance changes are available ad hoc at the
Studio's prevailing hourly rate, on the Studio's schedule — the Client
acknowledges that unmaintained software accrues risk, and that declining a
plan is a decision, recorded in writing.

**Either way:** the Studio will disclose actively exploited vulnerabilities
it becomes aware of in software it delivered, and hand over everything
needed for a third party to take maintenance on at any time.
