# Stage 09 — Giving them the keys
| Document | Goes to | When |
|---|---|---|
| `handover-template.md` | **Client** | At the handover session, filled in completely |

Everything in their name: domain, hosting, payment account, analytics, the
source code. The handover document is the thing they'll want in two years when
something needs changing and nobody remembers how it works — so write it for
that person, not for today.

The full list of what physically gets delivered, in what format, is in the
[roadmap](../../roadmap.md#what-they-actually-receive).

**Gate:** they can sign in to everything without you, and they've done it once
in front of you.
