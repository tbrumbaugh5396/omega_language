# Stage 08 — Going live
| Document | Goes to | When |
|---|---|---|
| `launch-checklist.md` | Internal, share the summary | Start it a week before, not on the day |

See also the [delivery, deployment & setup roadmap](../../roadmap.md) — the
launch checklist covers *whether* you're ready; the roadmap covers *how* the
deployment is actually done.

**Never launch on a Friday.** Never launch the day before you travel.

**Final payment before launch, never after.** Leverage disappears the moment
the site is live, and chasing money from a client who already has what they
wanted is its own small career.

**Gate:** live, verified, backed up, and paid.
