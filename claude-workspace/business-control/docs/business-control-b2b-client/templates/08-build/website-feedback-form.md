## How to give feedback that's fast to act on

**Be specific about where.** "The About page, third paragraph" beats
"somewhere on the site".

**Say what it should be, not just that it's wrong.** "Change 'we provide
solutions' to 'we fix leaks'" is one edit. "This copy isn't right" is a
conversation.

**Screenshots help enormously** for anything visual — especially on mobile,
where I need to know your device.

**Separate the must-fix from the nice-to-have.** I'll do all of it, but if
time gets tight I need to know what actually matters.
