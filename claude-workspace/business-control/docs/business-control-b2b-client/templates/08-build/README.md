consolidated response within five working days. A third is a paid add-on. This
one clause is the difference between a profitable studio and free work forever.

**Use the change order every time, warmly.** It takes two minutes. The cost of
*not* using it is the whole margin, collected in small friendly increments.

**Gate:** round 2 signed off and the final invoice raised.
