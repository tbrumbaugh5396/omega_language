## Before you start — please read

This is **round 1 of the 2 rounds included** in your project, and it's about
**direction**, not detail.

**What I'm asking about:** does this feel like your brand, is the structure
right, is the message landing?

**What to ignore for now:** typos, exact wording, final photography, small
spacing. All of that gets fixed in round 2 — flagging it twice wastes a pass
we could spend on something that matters.

**Please send one consolidated response.** Gather everyone's views internally
first. If feedback arrives in pieces I'll hold it politely until the round
closes, so nothing gets lost or done twice.

Take your time — but if I don't hear back within [10] working days I'll take
it as approved and move on, so the launch date holds.
