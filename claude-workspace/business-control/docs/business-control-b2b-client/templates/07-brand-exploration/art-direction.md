## What this document governs

Everything built from here refers back to this. A change that contradicts it —
a new colour, a second display face, a different photographic treatment — is a
change order, priced and agreed before it's built.

That isn't bureaucracy. It's the mechanism that stops a coherent brand becoming
an incoherent one through a series of small, individually reasonable requests.
