## Before you present

- [ ] Each direction shown **in context** — homepage, phone, and one physical
      thing if there is one
- [ ] Each has a name and a one-sentence argument
- [ ] Contrast checked at AA on every direction, not just the one you like
- [ ] Type licensing costed for every direction you're willing to have chosen
- [ ] No favourite plus two strawmen — you must be able to build any of them
- [ ] Your recommendation decided, and your reason written in one sentence

**My recommendation:** [DIRECTION] — because ______________________

> Recommend one, plainly, and say why. Presenting three options with no view is
> not neutrality; it's abdication, and it hands the decision to whoever in the
> room is loudest.
