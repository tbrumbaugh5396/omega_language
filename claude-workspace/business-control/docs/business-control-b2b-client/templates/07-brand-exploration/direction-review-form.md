## Before you look

**Judge the direction, not the details.** The words are placeholder, the
photographs are stand-ins, the spacing will be refined. What you're deciding is
the *character* — whether this is the right personality for your business.

**Ask "is this right?", not "do I like it?"** They're different questions, and
only the first one matters. The direction that's right for the business is
sometimes not the one you'd choose for your own living room.

**Show it to someone who isn't you.** Ideally a customer. Their two-second
reaction is worth more than an hour of your deliberation.

## What happens next

We write the chosen direction up as an **art direction** document — palette,
typography, imagery, motion, voice, with the rules for using them. You sign
that, and it becomes the reference for everything built afterwards.

From that point, changes that contradict it are change orders. Not to be
difficult — it's the thing that stops the site drifting into a different brand
by week nine, one small reasonable request at a time.
