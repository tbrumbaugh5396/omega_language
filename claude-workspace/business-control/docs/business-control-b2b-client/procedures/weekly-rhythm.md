# Procedure — the weekly rhythm

Thirty minutes, same time every week. 
It is the cheapest thing in this folder and it prevents most of what goes wrong.

---

## Every active client

Open `clients/[slug]/README.md` and update:

- [ ] Stage, and whether it's on schedule
- [ ] **Content received, as a percentage** — the number that predicts lateness
- [ ] Rounds used
- [ ] Change orders raised and their value
- [ ] Invoiced vs paid
- [ ] Biggest current risk, in one sentence

## Send them their roadmap

- [ ] Update `04-kickoff/to-client/project-roadmap.md` — phase, percentages, next thing they'll see, next thing you need
- [ ] Fill the "last updated" line with **one sentence on what changed**
- [ ] Send it, even in a quiet week

> A quiet week still gets a roadmap. Silence is the thing clients fill in with
> the worst available explanation, and one email prevents a fortnight of it.

## Chase, in writing

- [ ] Anything outstanding from the client, named and dated
- [ ] Anything you owe them, delivered or re-promised with a date

> **Put the stall in writing the week it starts, not the week it hurts.** A
> friendly note saying "we're waiting on the product photos, which moves launch
> by a week if they arrive after Friday" is a normal message on week two and an
> accusation on week seven. Written contemporaneously, it's also the evidence
> that the slip wasn't yours.

## Money

- [ ] Any invoice more than 7 days overdue — chase today
- [ ] Any milestone reached without an invoice raised — raise it

## Pipeline

- [ ] Proposals sent 3 and 10 days ago — follow up
- [ ] Consultations held with no proposal yet — write it

## Once a month, additionally

- [ ] Backups restore-tested for every hosted client
- [ ] Dependency and security updates applied
- [ ] Care plan renewals coming up in the next 90 days
- [ ] One procedure in this folder improved, based on what actually happened
