# Procedure — handing over

**When:** a week after launch, once it's proven stable.

**Takes:** a 90-minute session plus an hour of preparation.

---

## Before the session

- [ ] Fill in the [handover
      document](../templates/10-handover/handover-template.md) **completely** —
      no `[BRACKETS]` left
- [ ] Every account in their name; you as a user, not an owner
- [ ] Source code in their organisation
- [ ] Design files, brand assets and photography in their drive
- [ ] `data/config.json` into their password manager, not an email
- [ ] Check the full deliverables list in the
      [roadmap](../roadmap.md#what-they-actually-receive)

## The session — record it

Cover exactly these, in this order:

1. Add and edit a product, with a photo
2. Take, fulfil and refund an order
3. Edit a page and publish it
4. Add a staff member and choose what they can see
5. Where the numbers are, and which one to look at on a Monday
6. What to do when something breaks

**Have them drive.** 
Watching you do it teaches nobody anything. 
The person who will actually use it should complete each task themselves while you sit on your hands.

## At the end of the session

- [ ] Offer the [care plan](../templates/11-aftercare/care-plan-agreement.md) — now, while it's obvious how much there is to look after
- [ ] Offer a block of update hours as the alternative
- [ ] Say when the 30 days of support ends, and put it in writing
- [ ] Book the testimonial conversation for 3–4 weeks out

> **Sell the care plan today.** In two months they've forgotten how much they
> needed you, and a cold maintenance pitch is a far harder conversation than a
> warm one on the day you handed over something that works.

---

**Done when:** they signed in to everything themselves in front of you, the recording is in their drive, and the care plan is either signed or explicitly declined.
