# Aftercare

Blank masters: [`templates/11-aftercare/`](../../../templates/11-aftercare/) — that folder's
README says what gets sent and what opens the next gate.

## What lands here

| `to-client/` | `internal/` |
|---|---|
| Care plan, signed — or the decline, in writing | Backup restore tests, dated |
| Support, monitoring and security schedules, signed | Update log, month by month |
| The escalation contacts they gave you | Vulnerability scans and what you cleared |
| Monthly reports as sent | Incident notes, including the near ones |
| Growth retainer, if they took one | Annual compliance review findings |
| Testimonial request and what came back | Renewal date and when you'll raise it |

**The one that matters:** the care plan, signed or explicitly declined. A
"we'll think about it" left unrecorded becomes a client who believes they're
covered and isn't.

**The dated restore test is the one that saves you.** Every other line here is
paperwork; that one is the difference between an outage and a closure.

> Raise the renewal in month ten, not month twelve. A plan that lapses silently
> is a client you lost without either of you deciding to.

**`internal/` is never sent.** If it would be awkward to forward, it belongs
there.
