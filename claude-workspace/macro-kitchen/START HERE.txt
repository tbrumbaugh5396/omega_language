MacroKitchen — start here

1) Double-click:  command_utilities/Install MacroKitchen.command
   (one-time setup — builds a private .venv inside this folder)

2) Double-click:  command_utilities/Start MacroKitchen.command
   Your browser opens at http://127.0.0.1:8900

3) On first launch you'll see a landing page — click "Sign up" to create
   your account. Each account keeps its own private data on this machine.
   Your profile (top-right) lets you rename yourself, change your password,
   or log out.

Stop the server:  command_utilities/Stop MacroKitchen.command
Desktop app icon: command_utilities/Install App Icon.command
Uninstall:        command_utilities/Uninstall MacroKitchen.command
                  (keeps your data unless you say otherwise)

Want example content to explore first?
  Easiest: after signing up, the Today tab shows a "Load starter content"
  button while your account is empty — one click gives you a pantry of
  common ingredients, meal-prep recipes, and a few planned days.
  Or double-click: command_utilities/Seed Starter Content.command
  (same content; with several accounts, pass --user <name>)

Backup & restore live in the app itself: the Targets tab has a
"Data & backup" card to save everything to a JSON file or restore from one.

Suggested first session:
  · Targets tab — fill in your profile; watch your BMR, maintenance
    calories, and daily target compute live
  · Guide tab — the visual walk-through of energy balance, macros,
    the plate method, and the weekly meal-prep workflow
  · Plan tab — drop recipes onto the week, then generate a shopping list
  · Each day: log meals on the Log tab, tap water glasses on Today,
    and add a morning weigh-in — Body shows your trend vs. goal
